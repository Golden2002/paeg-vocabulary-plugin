phenomenon /fəˈnɒmɪnən/ noun 频次 37 · 全书第 42 B2
US /fəˈnɑːmənən/ UK /fəˈnɒmɪnən/ 复数 phenomena
现象；奇观；非凡的人（或事物）。可被感知、观察或记录的事实与事件，尤指不寻常或难以解释者；引申指因出色或轰动而引人注目的人或事物。
a fact or event that can be observed and studied, especially one that is unusual or difficult to explain; a person or thing that is very successful or impressive.
希腊语 phainomenon「显现之物」← phainein「使显现、照亮」
现象（可观察的事实或事件，尤指不寻常或难以解释者） a fact or event that can be observed, especially one that is unusual or difficult to explain 原书语境：批评文本将贝蒂·弗里丹命名的“女性迷思”归为社会现象
奇才；引起轰动的人或事物 a person or thing that is very successful or impressive 引申义：称某人某事为“现象”，意味着其引人注目、超乎寻常
义项 1.2 · 补充例句
A child prodigy who composes symphonies at seven is a rare phenomenon.七岁便能创作交响乐的神童，是罕见的现象级人物。
From Late Latin phaenomenon, from Greek phainomenon “that which appears or is seen,” neuter present participle of phainesthai “to appear,” passive of phainein “to bring to light, cause to appear.” Related to phantasm, epiphany, and diaphanous.

genotype /ˈdʒiːnətaɪp/ noun 频次 9 · 全书第 128 C1
US /ˈdʒiːnətaɪp/ UK /ˈdʒenətaɪp/ 对比 phenotype /ˈfiːnətaɪp/
基因型；遗传型。生物体所携带的整套遗传构成（与表现型 phenotype 相对）；比喻义指某事物表象之下的内在根源或底层编码。
the genetic constitution of an organism, as distinguished from its observable characteristics; figuratively, the underlying constitution or inner code of a thing.
希腊语 genos「出生、种类」+ typos「类型、印记」
基因型（遗传学标准义：决定生物遗传特性的基因构成） the set of genes in an organism that determines a particular characteristic 学科语境：与表现型相对——基因型是潜在的编码，表现型是显现的结果
引申义：事物的内在根源、底层编码（比喻用法） the underlying constitution or inner logic of something
义项 1.2 · 补充例句
The genotype of a culture determines which phenotypes—ideas, customs, institutions—it will express.一种文化的基因型，决定了它将表达出哪些表现型——观念、习俗与制度。
Coined in 1909 by the Danish botanist Wilhelm Johannsen, from German Genotypus, a blend of Greek genos “race, kind, birth” and typos “type, mark, model.” Johannsen introduced the term to distinguish the heritable constitution of an organism (genotype) from its visible form (phenotype).
genGr.「出生、种类」+-o-连接元音「复合词中缀」+typeGr.「类型、印记」
